NUMMAT - Think. Match. Clear.
=============================

START
  Double-click NUMMAT.exe. Keep NUMMAT.exe and the _internal folder together.
  Your players and progress are saved in the "saves" folder here, so copying
  this whole folder to another computer (or a pen drive) takes your progress along.

PLAYERS
  First time: create a player (a PIN is optional) or play as Guest.
  NUMMAT remembers who played last and logs them in automatically.
  To change player: click your avatar (top right) -> Switch player.

RULES
  Clear the board by matching pairs of tiles:
    * equal numbers         5 and 5
    * numbers that make 10  3 and 7, 1 and 9, 2 and 8, 4 and 6
  The two tiles must be connected by a clear line - only empty cells in between:
    * same row   * same column   * diagonal
    * row wrap: the last tile of a row joins the first tile of the next row
  Tap one tile, then its partner - or drag from one to the other.

GAME MODES
  Classic      the original, plus 6 themed level packs
  Chain        play a pair your last match opened to grow a chain multiplier
  Time Attack  as many matches as you can in 2 minutes
  Daily        one puzzle a day, the same for everyone - 20 moves, no hints
  Zen          no timer, no score, unlimited undo and hints
  Expert       walls, frozen tiles, wildcards (★ matches anything)

HELP WHILE PLAYING
  Hint (3 levels) - Why? - Best move - Undo - Solve (checks if the board can be cleared)
  The "Why this worked" panel explains every match you make.

Replay the tour any time: avatar menu -> How to play.

PROBLEMS?
  * "Windows protected your PC"  ->  click More info -> Run anyway.
  * Unzip first (right-click the zip -> Extract All) - don't run it from inside the zip.
  * If NUMMAT opens in an Edge window instead of its own, that's fine - it is the
    automatic backup mode for PCs where the normal window can't start.
  * Still stuck? Right-click the downloaded zip -> Properties -> tick "Unblock" -> OK,
    then extract again. Details of any start-up problem are in saves\nummat.log.
